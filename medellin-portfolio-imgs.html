The project

Barrio La Cruz sits high on Manrique's slope, part of the comuna network that Medellín's social urbanism model has slowly reshaped over two decades — library-parks, cable cars, and public infrastructure delivered to neighborhoods the city once treated as informal and unreachable.

This project sat inside that tradition at a small scale: working with residents to plan and build a community library that responds to the site's terrain, its drainage, and the way people already move through it. What follows is the record of that process, from the first measurements taken on the slope to the weeks spent laying block alongside the crew who will use the space.
